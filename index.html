Question 1 of 5

An initial reaction mixture contains 2 moles of reactant A. If 1 mole of reactant B is added and completely reacts, how many total product units are formed assuming 1:1 stoichiometry?

Hint: Recall that 1 mole of A reacts with 1 mole of B to form products directly.
Explanation: 1 mole A + 1 mole B = 2 total product units formed in a completed 1:1 reaction.
